
Young mouse process

### 01.RNA-integration:
Implements cross-platform integration and annotation of single-cell RNA data using Zeng_10x.RNA as reference, standardizing Joint-Cabernet.RNA datasets for cell-type mapping.

### 02.Joint-Cabernet.cellType_State: 
Cell typing through RNA analysis, and related direct analyses of DNA methylation and/or RNA expression.

### 03.subclass.DMR: 
Calling and characterization of differentially methylated regions (DMRs), with a particular focus on DMRs overlapping genes.

### 04.epigenetic.DHMR_3C: 
Integrated analyses of 3C data, DNA methylation data, and RNA expression data, revealing the potential regulatory roles of distal hyper-DHMRs.

### 05.epigenetic.DHMR_Histone:
Integrates histone modification and DNA methylation multi-omics to study distribution patterns and regulatory functions of histone marks in differential methylation regions.

### 06.epigenetic.GWAS:
Performs epigenome-wide association studies to identify genetic loci associated with hyperactive methylation regions and their roles in complex traits.
