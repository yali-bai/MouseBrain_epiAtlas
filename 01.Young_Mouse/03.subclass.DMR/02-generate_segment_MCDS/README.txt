"01-run_segment_mcds.sh" is first used for generating mcds data of the mean CG methylation levels for the segments in individual cells.
"02-get_h5ad_from_MCDS.ipynb" is then used to conver the mcds data into the format of h5ad for further processing.

Similar processes for generating the mean CG methylation levels for the segments on subclasses also used similar codes through application upon the merged allc files.
