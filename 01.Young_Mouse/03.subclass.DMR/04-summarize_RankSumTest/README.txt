"01-call_DMR.ipynb" is used for DMR-calling with results from the Wilcoxon rank-sum tests and the absolute differences of the mean methylation levels of the group differed from the robust means of all groups.
"02-check_DMR_number.ipynb" is used for count the numbers of the DMRs.
The files in "03-volcano_plot_neuron_1vsOthers" are for the plotting of volcano plots related to DMR-calling.
The files in "04-heatmap_for_DMRs" are for the plotting of the heatmaps of the DMRs.
The files in "05-stacked_barplot_for_DMR_number" are for the plotting of the stacked barplots of the numbers of the DMRs.
The files in "06-annotation_of_DMRs" are for the annotation of the DMRs and the plotting of the relevant pie-charts.
The files in "07-hyper_DHMR_bw" are for generating BigWig files of the hyper DHMRs, which can be visualized in IGV.
