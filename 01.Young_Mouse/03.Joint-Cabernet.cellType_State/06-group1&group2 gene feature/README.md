### 01. RNA_and_DNA_correlation.group1_group2_genes_length_distribution.share_y_axis_density_up_and_boxplot_down.R: 
This script is used to detect features (for example gene length and CpG number) of group1 and group2 genes and plot density plot (up) and boxplot (down) of log gene length and log CpG number of genes in group1 and group2 

### 02. group1_group2_genes_RNA_expression_across_subclasses_CV_SD.R: 
plot distribution of coefficients of variation (CVs) and standard deviations (SDs) of genes in Group 1 and in Group 2.
