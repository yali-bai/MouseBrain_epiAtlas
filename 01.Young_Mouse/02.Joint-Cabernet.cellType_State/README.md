### "01-DNA_methylation_ratio"
"01-DNA_methylation_ratio" is designed to show the levels of DNA methylation in different subclasses.

### "02-merge_allc"
"02-merge_allc" is for merging single-cell ALLC files into pseudo-bulk ALLC files.
First, allc tables recording the files to be merged, are generated.
Then, the actual merging processes are carried out using allcools.
Note that subclass-level merged allc files are generated by merging single-cell allc files,
while the class-level and threeclass-level merged allc files are merged from subclass-level merged allc files.

### "03-methylation_line_graph_of_gene_groups"
"03-methylation_line_graph_of_gene_groups" shows the methylation levels of RNA expression groups of genes. All genes were divided into three groups based on RNA expression: non-expression, low-expression and high-expression, and the methylation levels of the three groups of genes in the genebody and promoter regions were displayed.

### "04-Correlation_calculation"
"04-Correlation_calculation" calculated the correlation between RNA expression and methylation rate of genes in all cells.

### "05-ternary_plot"
"05-ternary_plot" plots the ternary plots (5hmCG, 5mCG, CG) colored by RNA expression.
30 representative genes from group 1 and group 2, are chosen.
Single-cell methylation information is plotted in ternary plots, with the RNA expression information coded in color.

density plot and boxplot of log gene length and log CpG number of genes in group1 and group2
