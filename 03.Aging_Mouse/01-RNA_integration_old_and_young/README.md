### 01. intergrate_with_some_region_of_zeng.using_selectable_markers.R: 
This script is used to 
(1) integrate Joint-Cabernet all samples (old and young) and Zeng 10X RNA by top1000 subclass markers of integration of Joint Cabernet young mouse and Zeng 10x RNA. 
(2) After integration and label transfer, we output allc files for all subclasses and ages, and output a meta-information containing QC, unique_id, celltype.
(3) UMAP or t-SNE plot of major class, subclass, age and data source distribution.
