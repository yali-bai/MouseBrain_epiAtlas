### 5hmCH.all_cells.global_methy.txt: 
global 5hmCH/5mCH_5hmCH level of all Joint-Cabernet cells. The first column is the cell name, and the second column is the global methylation level of 5hmCH.

### 5mCH_5hmCH.all_cells.global_methy.txt: 
global 5mCH_5hmCH level of all Joint-Cabernet cells. The first column is the cell name, and the second column is the global methylation level of 5mCH+5hmCH.

### global_DNA_old_vs_young_diff.CG.csv: 
The difference in the DNA global methylation rate of CG between the young and the old. 
The "lt_twice_subclass" column is the subclass of integration of Joint Cabernet and Zeng 10X. 
The "hmCG_fold_change", "mCG_fold_change" and "mCG_hmCG_fold_change" columns are the subclass mean hmCG, true_mCG, mCG+hmCG methylation levels of old mice divided by young mice. 
The "hmCG_diff", "mCG_diff", "mCG_hmCG_diff" columns are the subclass difference of old mice minus young mice.

### subclass_5hmC_chr_number.txt: 
The number of candidate DHMRs on each chromosome, used for batch partitioning when submitting DHMR significance tests. The fisrt column is the number of DHMRs of chromsome and the second column is the chromsome number.

### subclass_5mC_chr_number.txt: 
The number of candidate DMRs on each chromosome, used for batch partitioning when submitting DMR significance tests. The fisrt column is the number of DMRs of chromsome and the second column is the chromsome number.
